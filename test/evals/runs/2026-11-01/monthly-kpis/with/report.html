KPI report: December 2025

Revenue and customers rose, but churn hit its high for the year and NPS fell. Changes are from November 2025.

Revenue recovered for a second month

601,000 in December, up from 496,000 in October. The year totals 6.54 million.

Customers grew through the year

485 more than in January, a gain of 5.9%. The axis does not start at zero, so the climb looks steeper than it is.

Churn ended at its high for the year

3.3% in December, above the 3.2% of July and October. It started the year at 2.3%. Lower is better.

NPS is well off its April peak

34 in December against 51 in April. Only March, at 32, was lower.
