Monthly KPI report, December 2025

Revenue and customers rose in December, but churn hit its high of the year

Revenue rose 8.9% from November to 601,000, and customers reached 8,705, the most this year. The warning signs are in retention and sentiment: churn rose to 3.3%, its highest month of 2025, and NPS fell to 34, its second-lowest.

This is more than one bad month. October to December averaged 3.1% churn and an NPS of 36, against 2.4% and 42 for July to September.
